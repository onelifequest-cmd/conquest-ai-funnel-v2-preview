FAQ

Straight answers

Does Grace replace my team?
No. She answers and books what she can, then hands anything sensitive to a person on your team. You stay the owner of the relationship.
Whose phone number does she use?
Yours. Grace is built on your business number and accounts. You own the leads and the data.
Can she say the wrong thing to a client?
She's trained on your scripts and knows when to escalate. New automations are tested on a test contact and switched on only after you approve. Quiet hours and opt-out rules apply to texts.
Is this compliant for credit repair?
We design for credit-repair workflows and avoid guarantee language in outbound copy. Compliance features support — but don't replace — your own legal review. This is not legal advice.
